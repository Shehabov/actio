# BRAND.md

Machine-readable brand rules for Actio, a Lumofy product.

This file and `brand/Actio-Brand-Guidelines-v1.1.pdf` are versioned together. A change to one without the other is a defect. The PDF is the document for people and carries the reasoning; this file is the document for agents and carries the rules. Where they disagree, the PDF wins and this file is wrong.

- **Version:** 1.1
- **Date:** September 2026
- **Parent:** Lumofy. Actio replaces the retired Lumofy Engage sub-brand.

---

## 1. What the product is

Actio is the accountability layer for employee feedback. Every issue is classified by who has the authority to change it, assigned to a named owner with a date, and held open until evidence of the change is attached.

- **Category:** employee listening and action management, sold to operations as retention infrastructure rather than to HR as a culture programme.
- **Measured on:** first-90-day attrition, closure rate, median days to close. Never a sentiment score.
- **Target conditions:** small screens, second languages, shared devices, no corporate email. These are the default, not edge cases.
- **Launch locales:** Bahasa Indonesia, English, Tagalog. Arabic inherited from Lumofy.

### Principles, in precedence order when they conflict

1. **Protective.** Anonymity is engineered rather than asserted. Misconduct leaves the engagement workflow entirely and is handled as a case.
2. **Plain.** State mechanics rather than promises.
3. **Restrained.** One accent colour, one border weight, one motion curve.

Also: **accountable** (nothing closes without evidence), **honest about limits** (publish unflattering figures, say clearly when something cannot be changed), **built for the frontline**.

A decision none of these would have changed was an aesthetic decision and should be described as one.

## 2. Voice

### Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Case | Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings. Capitals only for proper nouns and Plex Mono labels at 12px. |
| Numbers | Numbers before adjectives. `41% responded (n=612)`, never "participation is strong". |
| Attribution | Name the person and the date. `Assigned to Dewi, due 14 March`. |
| Limits | State what cannot be done, with a reason. A stated limitation keeps people answering; silence does not. |
| Person | No first person. Actio has no name it speaks in. It is not a companion or a coach. |
| Punctuation | No exclamation marks in system copy. No emoji anywhere in the product. Em dashes sparingly, never more than one per paragraph. |
| Scope | Product, email, WhatsApp, sales material, website, support replies. There is no separate marketing voice. |

### Banned vocabulary

`empower` `seamless` `unlock` `leverage` `journey` `supercharge` `delight` `effortless` `revolutionise` `game-changing` `moments that matter` `listening strategy` `people leader`

Also not used in errors: `oops` `sorry` `unfortunately` `something went wrong` `please try again later` `an unexpected error occurred`

Use instead: `survey` `issue` `owner` `deadline` `closed`.

### Register by reader

| Reader | How to write |
|---|---|
| Employee | Shortest sentences. Describe the mechanism, not the intention. |
| Team lead | Lead with the action and the deadline. |
| Operations | Volume, trend, and where the load falls. |
| Executive | The number first, then the structural reading. |
| Errors | What happened, then the next step. No apology, no blame on the reader. |
| Privacy | Precise and unhurried. Never soften a limitation. |

### The check

If a competitor could publish the sentence unchanged, it carries no information.

### Approved and rejected

| Approved | Rejected |
|---|---|
| Assigned to Dewi, due 14 March | Action item successfully created! |
| 3 of 8 actions closed this cycle | You are crushing it this quarter |
| We cannot change pay bands this quarter. The budget is set until July. | We are looking into it |
| 41% responded (n=612) | Great engagement! |
| This goes to your site director | Escalating to leadership |
| Could not send. Check the number. | Oops, something went wrong |
| Your manager sees this grouped with 11 other responses | Your response is completely anonymous |
| Closed 12 days late. Evidence attached. | Completed |
| Nothing has changed here since March. | Keep up the momentum |
| Rosters are published under 48 hours before the shift. | Schedule communication is an opportunity area |

### Messaging hierarchy

Use the shortest form the context allows. Longer forms expand the same claim and never contradict it.

- **Tagline:** Feedback that closes.
- **One sentence:** Actio routes employee feedback to whoever can actually fix it, and does not let it close without proof.
- **Short form:** Engagement tools send every finding to the frontline manager, including pay, staffing, shift patterns and career paths, which that manager has no power to change. Actio routes each issue to whoever can change it, and it stays open until they do.
- **Boilerplate:** Actio is the employee listening and accountability product from Lumofy. It is built for high-attrition frontline operations in Southeast Asia and works over WhatsApp, SMS and web in Bahasa Indonesia, English and Tagalog.

Never lead with artificial intelligence. It describes how the product works, not what it is for.

## 3. The seal

Reuleaux triangle with a circular aperture, `fill-rule: evenodd`, no gradient.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Grid | 100 × 100 units |
| Circumradius | 33 |
| Vertices | −90°, 30°, 150° on the circumcircle |
| Side and arc radius | 57.158 (R3) |
| Arc centres | Each arc centred on the opposite vertex |
| Optical centre | 50, 54.4 |
| Aperture | r15 at 50, 53.4 |
| Thinnest wall | 13.6 units |

Do not redraw. The arcs are mathematically defined and an eyeballed version is measurably wrong. Use the supplied files in `brand/logo/`.

### Lockups

| Lockup | Use |
|---|---|
| Horizontal | Primary. Almost all cases. |
| Vertical | Square spaces only. |
| Seal alone | App icons, favicons, avatars. |
| Parent (Lumofy Actio) | First commercial use, contracts, site footer. |

Wordmark is Source Sans 3 SemiBold at `-0.025em`, supplied as outlines. Never set as live text. Gap between mark and wordmark is one aperture diameter, derived from the mark, never a typed pixel value.

### Clear space and size

- Clear space: one aperture diameter on every side, 30 units at mark scale. Nothing enters it, including type, rules and other marks.
- Minimum: 16px or 6mm seal, 84px or 24mm horizontal lockup.
- Below 24px: ship the supplied raster, do not scale the vector. The aperture fills on low-density screens.

### Approved grounds

White, Halo, Ink 50, Cosmos, Vega 400. No other colour, including sibling accents.

- On Cosmos: white or Vega. Vega on Cosmos is 8.62:1.
- On Vega: seal and wordmark in Cosmos. **White on Vega is 2.27:1 and must not be used.**
- On photography: place the lockup on a solid plate. The mark is never set directly over an image.

### Prohibited

Rotate, stretch, outline, shadow, recolour outside the approved list, fill the aperture, add a container, gradient, crop, use as a bullet or repeating pattern, animate beyond the approved reveal, place over a photograph without a plate, redraw in another application.

## 5. Typography

Self-hosted WOFF2, subset to Latin, Latin Extended and Arabic. Never a public CDN: a blocked request means an unreadable survey. `font-display: swap` with metric-matched fallbacks.

| Face | Use | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Source Sans 3 | Headings only | 600, 400. Never 300, never 800. |
| IBM Plex Sans | All body and interface text | 400, 500, 600. Never 300, it collapses on low-cost Android. |
| IBM Plex Mono | Every number, plus 12px labels, identifiers, timestamps, captions | 400, 500 |
| IBM Plex Sans Arabic | All Arabic | Regular, Medium, SemiBold |

Source Sans 3 fallback is Georgia, which is visibly wrong and therefore gets fixed. No other substitution is permitted.

Every number is Plex Mono with tabular figures: response rates, days to close, counts, dates, case identifiers, currency. A monospace numeral reads as instrumentation.

### Scale

| Role | Face and weight | Size / line height |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Source Sans 3 600 | 40 / 46 |
| H1 | Source Sans 3 600 | 30 / 38 |
| H2 | Source Sans 3 600 | 22 / 30 |
| H3 | Plex Sans 600 | 18 / 26 |
| Body | Plex Sans 400 | 15 / 24 |
| Small | Plex Sans 400 | 13 / 20 |
| Caption | Plex Sans 500 | 12 / 16 |
| Metric | Plex Mono 500 | 28 / 32 |
| Label | Plex Mono 400 | 12 / 16 |

Tracking: `-0.015em` from 22px, `-0.03em` above 40px. Plex Mono labels at 12px take `0.02em`.

### Rules

| Always | Never |
|---|---|
| Sentence case | All caps, except Plex Mono labels at 12px |
| 68 character maximum measure | Title case anywhere, including buttons |
| Plex Mono, tabular, for numerals | Letterspaced lowercase |
| Left aligned, never justified | Weight 300, use 600 instead |
| Body weights 400 and 600 only | Italic for emphasis |
| Headings: no single word on the last line | Underline, except links in running text |

Constrain the container rather than reducing the type size. Check every screen at 200% browser zoom; nothing may clip or overlap.

## 6. Layout

| Breakpoint | Columns | Gutter | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile | 4 | 16px | 16px |
| Tablet | 8 | 20px | 32px |
| Desktop | 12 | 24px | 64px, 1440 maximum |

Breakpoints: 480, 768, 1024, 1440. Reading width 720px. Data width 1200px. Baseline 8px.

**Design the 360px view first.** Most Actio sessions happen on a phone; the desktop layout inherits from mobile.

### Spacing

Base unit 4px. Scale: `4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64`. Defaults: 8 inside a component, 16 between components, 24 between groups, 48 between sections.

**14, 18, 20 and 30 do not exist in this product.** Eight values force consistent decisions; a continuous scale produces drift.

### Radius and elevation

| Element | Radius |
|---|---|
| Buttons, inputs, selects | 8px |
| Cards | 12px |
| Modals | 16px |
| Pills and tags | 999px |
| Images | 8px, or none when full bleed |
| Single-sided borders | 0, always |

Nothing in the document flow carries a shadow. Cards separate by a hairline and by space. Only overlays lift, with a single shadow token. Rounded corners on an element bordered on one side only always read as a mistake: a left accent rule has a radius of zero.

## 7. Components

### Buttons

Variants: primary (`Assign owner`), secondary (`View evidence`), tertiary (`Cancel`, dismissive only), danger (`Reopen issue`, escalating or irreversible). One primary per view.

Height 40px, 48px on touch. Padding `8px 16px`. Radius 8px. Label is verb first, one to three words, and states what will happen: `Assign owner`, not `OK`, `Submit` or `Continue`.

Avoid disabled states. Keep the control enabled and explain on activation: a disabled button is low contrast and shows no tooltip on touch, so a frontline user cannot tell why it will not respond. Reopening, escalating and deleting are never the primary action on a view. Loading replaces the label with a spinner at the same width; the button does not resize.

### Inputs

Labels always above the field, never inside it. Placeholder text disappears the moment someone types. Placeholders show a real example of valid input, do not repeat the label, and are not prefixed with "e.g.".

Validation on blur, not on keystroke, with the message inline beside the field. No summary at the top of the form and no red border without an explanation.

Touch targets minimum 48 × 48px. The zero-to-ten scale is the most tapped control in the product and is used one-handed.

### Queue and status

The left rule shows who owns the item. The pill shows where it has reached. These are never merged into a single colour.

Sort order: overdue first, then in progress by deadline, then open, then closed. **Never sort by severity:** severity is a judgement and a deadline is a fact.

Protected rows carry no title, no detail and no assignee. The row appears so the count reconciles and nobody wonders where an item went.

Dates in Plex Mono, right aligned, so a long queue scans vertically.

### Cards

An issue is an object and takes a card. A queue is a list and takes rows. Forty issues rendered as cards cannot be scanned.

A card never contains another card. Where content inside a card needs grouping, use a hairline and space. Every card carries its identifier in Plex Mono at the lower right. Metric cards have no border and no card chrome; they are readings rather than objects.

### Notices

- **Empty state:** an invitation. Never opens with an apology or with "nothing here yet".
- **Toast:** states a fact in the past tense, with one undo affordance when the action is reversible. It does not congratulate.
- **Modal:** interrupts for irreversible decisions only. Title asks the question, body states the consequence, primary action repeats the verb from the title.

### Tables

Header Plex Sans 500 at 9px, Ink 500, one rule beneath. Row rule Ink 150 hairline, **no zebra striping**. Row height 44px, 52px on touch. Numeric columns right aligned, Plex Mono, tabular. Text columns left aligned, truncated with a tooltip. Sort one column at a time, direction shown by a chevron. Empty cell is an en dash, never blank and never zero. One density only.

### Charts

Vega 400 is the primary series, Ink 500 the comparison, Vega 200 the secondary. No other colour appears in a chart, and the Lumofy accents are never a categorical scale. Sample size is always shown beside a percentage. Axis labels and all figures in Plex Mono with tabular figures.

Not permitted: pie charts above three slices, three-dimensional effects, dual axes, truncated value axes. The comparison is shown even when it is unflattering.

### Icons

Line only, 1.5px stroke on a 24px grid, round caps and joins. Tabler or Lucide as the base, never both on one screen. Filled icons only for the active navigation state. An icon is never the sole carrier of meaning; pair with a label.

**No sparkle, star or wand. Actio does not advertise its AI.**

### Navigation

Five destinations, flat: Queue, Cycles, People, Evidence, Settings. No second level. The queue opens first, filtered to items owned by the person signed in. On mobile the same five as a bottom bar, labels always shown, never icons alone. The Lumofy wordmark and product switcher live in the account area, and that is the only place Sirius appears.

## 8. Motion

| Scope | Duration |
|---|---|
| Micro | 120ms |
| Panel | 200ms |
| Maximum | 300ms |

One curve throughout: `cubic-bezier(.2, 0, .2, 1)`. No spring, no overshoot. Reduced motion respected on every transition without exception.

Transform and opacity only. Animating layout properties causes reflow on the low-cost devices this product runs on.

**Never animates:** numbers counting up, charts drawing themselves, skeleton shimmer, and anything that delays information reaching someone who is mid-shift.

**The mark:** one approved reveal, the aperture scales from 0.9 to 1 over 200ms on the standard curve, outer form static, never repeated. Application launch, the first frame of a video, and the loading state of the web application only. The seal is never a spinner; loading uses a plain indeterminate bar in Vega.

## 9. Dark mode

Not an inversion. Cosmos is the page. Surfaces separate by moving to a lighter grey rather than by casting a shadow, because a shadow is invisible against a near-black ground.

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Page | `#0C0C0C` |
| Card | `#1A1A18` |
| Rule | `#232320` |
| Row rule | `#1C1C1A` |
| Primary text | `#EFEFEF` |
| Secondary text | `#A8A8A4` |
| Muted text | `#6E6E69` |
| Accent | `#00BFC4`, unchanged |
| Accent text | `#6FE0E5` (Vega 200 replaces Vega 600) |
| Borders | `#33332F` replaces Ink 150 |
| Chart grid lines | `#242422` |

Shadows removed entirely. Chart series unchanged. Photography unchanged, no darkening filter.

Status in dark mode is a 5px indicator dot with the label in secondary text. Filled status chips add saturated colour to every row and make a queue harder to scan.

**Never pure white.** Halo at `#EFEFEF` reduces halation on OLED screens read in darkness, which is when night-shift workers use this product.

Both modes are verified on every screen before release. A colour that works in only one mode is not part of the system. Follow the operating system preference by default, with an override that persists **per device rather than per account**, because a worker may use a shared handset.

## 10. Numbers, dates and names

| Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Percentages | Whole numbers. One decimal only below 10%. |
| Sample size | Always shown as `n=612` beside a percentage. |
| Change | Percentage points, written `+4pp` or `-4pp`. |
| Counts | Thousands separated by a comma in English, a full stop in Indonesian. |
| Currency | `Rp 2.450.000` in Indonesian, `IDR 2,450,000` in English. |
| Dates | `14 Mar 2026`. Never numeric only. |
| Relative time | Used up to 7 days, then absolute. |
| Deadlines | Absolute date plus days remaining. |
| Time zone | The site time zone, labelled, never the reader's. |
| Ranges | `1–14 Mar`, en dash, no spaces. |
| Names | Given name and family name as supplied. No reordering. Single names supported; never require a family name. |
| Names in assignments | Given name and initial: `Dewi R.` |
| Names in evidence | Given name and family name as supplied. |
| Never | Full name as recorded in the source system. Auto-generated initials as an avatar substitute. |
| Truncation | At the end with an ellipsis, full value on hover and focus. Never in the middle of a name. |

## 11. Error and edge copy

State what happened, then what to do. Two sentences at most, no apology, never blame the reader. `Could not send` is correct; `you entered an invalid number` is not. Every message names the next action, and where that action is possible from the screen the control appears beside the message.

| State | Copy |
|---|---|
| No connection | We could not reach the server. Your answers are saved on this device and will send when you are back online. |
| Send failed | Could not send to +62 812 3456 7890. Check the number and try again. |
| Group too small | Fewer than 5 people answered. Nothing from this group will be shown. |
| Cycle closed | This cycle closed on 14 March. Your answers were not recorded. |
| No permission | You can see items assigned to you and to your teams. This one belongs to another site. |
| Evidence missing | This item cannot be closed until a file or a note is attached. |
| Session expired | You have been signed out. Sign in again to continue where you were. |
| Nothing to show | No items are overdue. The next check-in goes out on 21 March. |

## 12. The privacy preview

Ahead of every first response in a cycle, and available from a persistent link in every subsequent message.

States the size of the group a response will be pooled into, the smallest group the system will report on, the fields a manager can filter by, and what happens to free text. Every figure is computed for the reader in front of it; an illustrative number would be a claim rather than a disclosure.

Tone is flat and mechanical. Any warmth here reads as persuasion, and persuasion is what the reader is guarding against. No card and no shadow: a rule and a set of values carry the whole screen.

## 13. Accessibility

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Target size | 48 × 48px minimum, including in tables |
| Focus order | Follows reading order. Every interactive element reachable by keyboard. |
| Focus ring | Always visible. Never removed, never replaced by colour alone. |
| Labels | Every input has a persistent visible label. |
| Icons | `aria-hidden` when decorative, `aria-label` when the only control. |
| Reduced motion | Honoured on every transition. |
| Timeouts | No session or form timeout inside a survey. |
| Auto-advance | Never. The reader controls progression. |
| Animation | Nothing loops and nothing flashes. |
| Language | Set the document language attribute per locale; mark inline language changes. |
| Colour vision | Every status carries a written label as well as a colour. |

Standard is WCAG 2.2 AA with the contrast check running in continuous integration rather than as a manual review. Before each release: keyboard-only traversal of every flow, a screen reader pass on the survey and the queue, and a check at 200% zoom. Test on a low-cost Android handset with a small screen. That device is the majority case.

## 14. Arabic and localisation

### Wordmark

Actio is transliterated, not translated, because a product name is a proper noun. In right-to-left settings the seal sits to the **right** of the wordmark so the mark is encountered first. **The seal itself does not mirror:** the Reuleaux triangle is symmetrical about its vertical axis. Earlier guidance to mirror the mark was incorrect.

Set in IBM Plex Sans Arabic SemiBold with no tracking. Gap is one aperture diameter, matching the Latin lockup. Minimum lockup height 18px; below that use the seal alone.

### Typography

Set Arabic one to two points larger than the equivalent Latin size. Increase line height by 15 to 20% over the Latin setting. Regular for body, Medium for interface labels, SemiBold for headings. **Never synthesise a bold.** There is no italic in Arabic and no capitalisation. Never letterspace: Arabic letters join and tracking breaks the connections. Do not use kashida elongation to justify; set ragged.

### Mirroring

Mirror what carries reading direction. Leave alone what carries physical or numerical direction.

| Element | Mirrors |
|---|---|
| Layout and columns | Yes |
| Lockup order | Yes, seal moves to the right |
| Directional icons: arrows, chevrons, back and forward, indentation, progress | Yes |
| Progress and sliders | Yes, fill originates from the right |
| The seal | No |
| Non-directional icons: clock, search, calendar, lock, chart, user | No |
| Numerals and figures | No |
| Charts | No. Value axes keep their conventional direction; labels move to the right. |
| Media controls | No. Play and skip follow the tape, not the script. |
| Phone numbers and code | No. Always left to right. |

Use logical CSS properties throughout: `inline-start` and `inline-end`, `margin-inline`. A layout built this way mirrors without a second stylesheet. Every screen is reviewed in Arabic before release, not translated after layout is signed off.

### Writing

Modern Standard Arabic for all product copy; dialect is not used because the workforce spans several and none is neutral. Western Arabic figures throughout, in Plex Mono. Gregorian dates. Latin product names, identifiers and phone numbers stay in Latin script and left-to-right order inside an Arabic sentence. Arabic copy is written against the same standards as English: a literal translation of a soft English sentence produces a soft Arabic one. Every string is read by a native speaker on a physical device before release.

### Indonesian and Tagalog

Bahasa Indonesia runs 15 to 20% longer than English and Tagalog can run further. Design every label and button to absorb the longest string rather than the English one. Arabic sets around 15% shorter than English.

**Never concatenate a string containing a count.** Indonesian and Tagalog form plurals differently from English and the result will be wrong in a way that nobody on the team can read.

Support users with a single legal name. Many Indonesian employees have one, and a form that demands a surname will exclude them.

**No idioms, metaphors or wordplay in any locale.** The reader is frequently on a small screen, in a second language, in a noisy room, between tasks.

## 15. Channels

WhatsApp messages carry **no logo, no colour and no header image**. The employee already knows who sent it, and every decorative byte is billed.

Submit templates as **utility rather than marketing**. Utility is roughly six to eight times cheaper in Indonesia. The category is decided by the platform, not by us, and it moves unit economics directly. Billing has been per message since July 2025, so structured exchanges beat chatty ones.

The privacy mechanic appears in the first message, before any question.

Closing the loop uses three headings: what we heard, what we are doing, what we cannot do.

## 16. Imagery

Documentary rather than staged. Real working environments: warehouse floors, contact centre rows, shift handovers, depot yards. Where someone appears they are working; no posed portraits and no eye contact with the camera. As shot, with no duotone, no brand-colour wash and no grading toward teal and orange. Crop wide so the environment reads as the subject.

Product imagery is real screenshots with names redacted, never a mockup containing placeholder text. Where something needs explaining, draw the mechanism; the diagrams in the guidelines are the house style.

## 17. Off brand

The current default aesthetic for AI software is the fastest available route to looking interchangeable. **If a screenshot could be dropped into a generic software landing page without anyone noticing, it has failed.**

Not used: purple to blue gradients, mesh gradients and aurora blurs, glassmorphism and frosted panels, glow, neon edges, coloured shadows, three-dimensional blobs and spheres, isometric illustration, illustrated mascots, stock photography of laughing colleagues, character art, spot illustration, terracotta and amber accents, confetti and celebration states, emoji in the interface, numbers that count up on load, skeleton shimmer, tinted callout boxes, rounded corners on every container, sparkle and wand icons for AI, neon accents on dark marketing pages, Inter, Poppins and Montserrat.

Required instead: flat fills, hairline borders, generous space, one accent used sparingly, monospace numerals, diagrams in place of illustration, and sentences that name a person and a date.

On terracotta specifically: the retired Engage orange sat eight degrees from the accent now associated with a widely used AI assistant. Keeping it would have read as derivative to precisely the buyers this product needs to convince.
